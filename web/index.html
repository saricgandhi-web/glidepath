      Method and sources

      How the simulation works

      Every year, stock and bond returns are drawn from correlated lognormal distributions calibrated to institutional capital market assumptions. The portfolio shifts from stocks toward bonds as you approach retirement, fees come out of each year's return, and everything is converted to today's dollars.

      While you work, you contribute your savings rate. In retirement, the portfolio pays whatever spending Social Security doesn't cover. A scenario succeeds if every year of spending is paid in full through your plan-to age.

      The three levers are solved by bisection on the same set of simulated markets, so applying one lands exactly on the target.

      Privacy

      Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored, and the page makes no network requests.

      Limits

      Taxes, pensions, home equity and one-off goals are not modeled. Social Security is estimated from your income with the SSA formula; your own statement at ssa.gov is more accurate. This is an educational tool, not investment advice.
